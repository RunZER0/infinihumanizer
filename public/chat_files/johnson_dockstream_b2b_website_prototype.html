Core Functionality

Purpose-Built for Mid-Market Cross-Dock Operators

Replace paper manifests, radio chatter, and whiteboard tracking with automated digital dispatch.

📲

Geofenced Driver Check-In

Drivers trigger automatic check-in when entering your facility perimeter. No mobile app download required—drivers receive an SMS link with door assignment and terminal guidelines.

🚪

Dynamic Dock Door Assignment

Algorithms allocate bays based on trailer temperature class, liftgate requirements, forklift staging proximity, and priority dispatch schedules.

Sign-on-Glass Electronic BOL

Drivers inspect master carton tallies and sign digital bills of lading directly on mobile glass in under 30 seconds. PDFs instantly sync to carrier and ERP records.

Shift Throughput Analytics

Multilingual Driver Interface
