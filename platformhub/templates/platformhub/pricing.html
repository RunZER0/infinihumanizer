Commercial events

A receipt does not have to equal a published package.

A payment can be product access, a one-time service, a deposit, a mobilisation payment, a milestone, a balance, or a monthly retainer. The invoice and project record explain which one it is.

Under USD 500

Focused managed work is normally paid in full after the scope is accepted.

USD 500–4,999

A 50% project deposit starts production; the remaining value is invoiced at the agreed final milestone.

USD 5,000+

A 30% mobilisation payment starts the engagement, with the rest billed against named milestones.

Monthly work

Retainers are billed at the start of each service month because capacity is being reserved.
