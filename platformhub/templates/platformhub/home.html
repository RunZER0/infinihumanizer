Research & production

Writing is only one stage.

The studio can enter before the first source is found or after a draft already exists. Research, review, synthesis, production, formatting and QA are parts of one pipeline.

Discover

Define the question, decision, audience and constraints before collecting material.

Research

Gather the right evidence: market, competitor, technical, review, source, product or other desk research.

Produce

Assure
