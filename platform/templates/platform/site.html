02 / WHY THIS MODEL

Writing is often the last 20% of the job.

The hard part may be finding the evidence, reviewing what already exists, deciding what matters, resolving contradictions, shaping the argument or turning it into something a specific audience can act on.

DISCOVER

Market, desk, technical, source and review research.

ANALYZE

Compare evidence, identify patterns and make the reasoning visible.

SYNTHESIZE

Turn scattered information into a coherent position or recommendation.

CREATE

Reports, decks, proposals, white papers, documentation and product language.

REVIEW

Originality, sources, structure, consistency, readability and final QA.
